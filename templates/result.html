❤️ Health Awareness

Maintaining healthy daily habits can support overall heart health. Some general health-awareness practices include:

  • 🏃 Stay physically active regularly according to your age and ability.
  • 🥗 Follow a balanced diet with vegetables, fruits, whole grains and appropriate protein.
  • 🚭 Avoid smoking and tobacco products.
  • 🩺 Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol when recommended.
  • 😴 Get adequate and consistent sleep.
  • 🧘 Manage stress through healthy activities and relaxation.
  • 💧 Maintain healthy hydration and regular meals.
  • 👨‍⚕️ Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have health concerns or abnormal test results.

These suggestions are provided for general health awareness only. This AI-assisted assessment is not a medical diagnosis and should not replace professional medical advice.
