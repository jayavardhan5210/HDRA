About the Project

Heart Disease Risk Assessment Agent is a Deep Learning-based system designed to assist in the early assessment of heart disease. The system analyzes patient health information and ECG signals to generate an AI-assisted health assessment and support early health awareness.

What We Do

👤

Patient Data Collection

Collect relevant patient health information such as age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI and other health parameters.

📈

ECG Signal Analysis

Allow users to upload ECG signals or reports for further analysis using Deep Learning techniques.

🧠

Deep Learning

Use Artificial Neural Networks for patient health data and Convolutional Neural Networks for ECG signal analysis.

📊

Health Assessment

Analyze the model outputs and provide an AI-assisted assessment of the user's heart health.

💡

Explainable Results

Present important factors contributing to the assessment in a simple and understandable format.

❤️

Early Health Awareness

Support early awareness and encourage users to seek appropriate professional medical guidance when needed.
